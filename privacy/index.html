Same Same! — a matching game for toddlers, for Android.

Privacy Policy — Same Same!

Last updated: 2026-09-03
Applies to the Android app com.nikitapetrovapps.samesame.

Summary

Same Same! collects no data at all. It does not transmit anything, shows no advertising, contains no analytics and asks for no account. The app does not even hold the Android INTERNET permission, so there is nowhere for data to go. Everything the game remembers stays in its own private folder on the device.

What the game stores on the device

  • which difficulty step the child is on, and which world was open last time;
  • how many pairs have been found with each animal — this is what fills the album;
  • how the child dressed the animals in the album;
  • counters of rounds, missed taps and playing time per day, used to draw the chart in the parents' menu;
  • the interface language and whether sound is on.

These records contain no name, age, photo, voice, location or device identifier — nothing that points at a person. They are removed together with the app, and also by Start over in the parents' menu.

What the game does NOT collect

  • No personal data: no name, e-mail, phone number, address or date of birth.
  • No accounts, no sign-in, no cloud saves.
  • No analytics, telemetry, trackers or advertising IDs.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data, and no data sharing — there is no data to share.
  • No chat, comments, user-generated content or any other communication between people.

Permissions

The app requests no Android runtime permissions — no camera, microphone, location, contacts or file access — and the released build ships without the INTERNET permission.

Links out of the app

The parents' menu — reached only after solving a simple addition problem, so a child does not open it by accident — has a page listing the research the game is built on. Tapping a source opens the publisher's website in the device browser. Those websites are run by other people and have their own privacy policies.

Apart from that page, the game contains no outbound links, and there are none anywhere in the part of the game a child plays.

Children

The game is made for children roughly between one and five years old. It is designed to meet the Google Play Families policy, the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the GDPR rules on children's data. Since nothing is collected, transmitted or stored outside the device, there is no personal data of a child to protect, disclose or delete on request.

The only place the game speaks to an adult is the parents' menu behind the addition gate, and nothing from there leaves the device.

Purchases

As of this revision the app contains no in-app purchases. If a purchase is added later, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play: the developer neither receives nor stores card numbers, billing addresses or any other payment details, and only learns whether this installation owns the item. This page will be updated before any purchase ships.
