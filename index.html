Find the two animals that are exactly alike

Same Same! is a quiet matching game for children from about one to five. Animals stand on the screen, two of them are the same — find them, and they wave and walk off.

What it is

There is not a single word in the game — spoken or written. The player cannot read yet, and a voice would speak the wrong language anyway. Instead there are animals that answer to a touch, and a melody that climbs one step with every pair found, so a child hears how close the round is to its end without being able to count.

A pair is not simply "two of the same animal": it is two that match in everything at once — the animal, the hat, and what it holds. Later on, two identical kittens appear in different hats, and sameness has to be found in one detail rather than in the whole picture.

How it is built

Difficulty adjusts itself

One long ladder for the whole game. A clean round steps up, two hard ones in a row step back down. No level picker: a two-year-old will not choose, and a parent cannot guess.

An album of animals

Every animal found appears little by little in the album, where it can be looked at closely, listened to, and dressed in the same hats that tell pairs apart in the game.

A parents' section

Behind a simple addition problem: what to expect at which age, the research the game is based on, progress charts, language and sound.

Private by design

No ads, no analytics, no accounts, no internet permission. Everything the game remembers stays in its own folder on the device.
